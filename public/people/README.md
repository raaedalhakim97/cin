# Photographs for the "people" slideshow on the landing page

Three files, referenced by `src/components/marketing/PeopleSlides.jsx`:

    team-1.jpg    team-2.jpg    team-3.jpg

Landscape, at least 1200x800, saved as `.jpg`. Much larger than that only costs
your visitors mobile data — a 4000px photo is not sharper on a phone.

Until the files exist the slideshow still renders: each slide shows its caption
over the mint gradient. That is a finished-looking state, not a broken one, so
there is no rush and nothing to hide in the meantime.

## Licensing — the part that actually matters

Only use images you are allowed to use commercially. A stock photo taken from a
search result and put on a company website is an invoice waiting to arrive, and
the fact that it was easy to download is not a defence.

Free, commercial use allowed, no attribution required:

  * https://unsplash.com/license
  * https://pexels.com/license

Search terms that suit this product better than "business meeting":
`warehouse team`, `construction site uae`, `retail staff dubai`,
`office team middle east`.

## Choosing well

Real working environments beat conference rooms. The rest of this site has been
careful not to overclaim — no invented customers, no invented statistics — and
four people in suits laughing at a laptop is the fastest way to undo that.
Pictures of the people who will actually clock in with the app are both more
honest and more persuasive.

Faces should be recognisable but the photo should not look posed for camera.
Avoid anything with a visible competitor logo or a legible screen.
